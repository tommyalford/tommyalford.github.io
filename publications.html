Publications

Selected publications

Papers with major contributions. The contribution is noted under each entry.

  • The Atacama Cosmology Telescope: Passband Measurements with an Analysis of Systematic Errors T. Alford et al. Submitted to Applied Optics (2026) [arXiv] [PDF] Led the analysis; identified and corrected FTS systematic errors, providing calibrated passbands and uncertainties for ACT DR6.
  • The Simons Observatory: Commissioning of the Mid-Frequency Small Aperture Telescopes K. Harrington, R. Gerras, N. Galitzki, S. Day-Weiss, Y. Sueno, T. Alford, et al. Preprint (2026) [arXiv] [PDF] Led the initial planet beam-mapping and calibration campaign for the first 90/150 GHz SAT; provided the passband measurements used in the analysis.
  • Simons Observatory: Predeployment Performance of a Large Aperture Telescope Optics Tube in the 90 and 150 GHz Spectral Bands C. Sierra, K. Harrington, S. Sutariya, T. Alford, et al. ApJS 276, 31 (2025) Led FTS spectral characterization, measured optical crosstalk, and characterized detector noise stability.
  • Laboratory Beam Characterization for the First Small Aperture Telescope R. Gerras, T. Alford, et al. Proc. SPIE 13094 (2024) Led holography beam measurements, constraining the near- and far-field beam size, ellipticity, and sidelobe amplitude.
  • The Simons Observatory: Design, Integration, and Testing of the Small Aperture Telescopes N. Galitzki et al. (including T. Alford) ApJS (2024) Led holography beam measurements for the first SAT; built the FTS and developed its spectral-measurement software.
  • Simons Observatory: Characterizing the Large Aperture Telescope Receiver with Radio Holography G. Chesmore et al. (including T. Alford) Applied Optics (2022) Contributed to beam measurement analysis software for radio holography characterization of the LAT receiver.
